## 👋 About This Project

This project explores common cybersecurity risks and simple ways to stay safer online.

The goal is to build a better understanding of cybersecurity through practical examples and security awareness.

---

## 🔎 Common Security Risks

| 🔐 Risk | 💡 What does it mean? |
|---|---|
| 🎣 **Phishing** | Fake messages or websites designed to trick users into sharing information. |
| 🔑 **Weak Passwords** | Simple or reused passwords can make accounts easier to compromise. |
| 🧠 **Social Engineering** | Manipulating people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions. |
| 🔗 **Unsafe Links** | Suspicious links may lead to fake or harmful websites. |

---

## 🛡️ How to Stay Safe

### 🟢 Protect Your Accounts

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Avoid reusing passwords.

### 🟡 Be Careful Online

- Check links before opening them.
- Be careful with unexpected messages.
- Avoid downloading files from unknown sources.

### 🔴 Protect Your Information

- Avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.
- Keep your software and operating system updated.
- Review your account security settings regularly.
